ERA 01 · THE EARLY UNIVERSE

The first light

About 380,000 years after the Big Bang, the universe cooled enough for electrons and nuclei to combine into neutral atoms. Light could finally travel freely. We observe this ancient afterglow today as the cosmic microwave background.

OBSERVATORY GUIDE

Explore at your own pace.

ORBIT is an educational visualization. Its planets use mapped surface textures and physically lit 3D spheres. The display compresses astronomical distances and exaggerates planet sizes so every world remains visible.

  • Drag to rotate your viewpoint; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Select a planet in the scene or use the navigation strip.
  • Adjust simulation speed, pause orbital motion, or hide orbit guides.
  • Use Reset to return to the full-system view.
